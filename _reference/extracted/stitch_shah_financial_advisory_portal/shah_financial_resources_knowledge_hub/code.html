[Article title]: How Hyderabad families can restructure fragmented mutual fund portfolios

lightbulb

Key Takeaway for Investors

Holding more than 7 to 9 equity mutual fund schemes rarely provides incremental diversification. Instead, it leads to accidental index-hugging, redundant expense ratios, and cumbersome operational overhead when filing annual tax returns.

Worked Example: Restructuring a ₹50 Lakh Domestic Equity Portfolio

Consider a family portfolio with 18 distinct fund schemes accumulated over [X years in practice]. By consolidating overlapping large-cap and multi-cap schemes, the annual Total Expense Ratio (TER) is systematically reduced:

Prior Fragmented Structure

18 Schemes (₹50,00,000)

Average weighted TER: 1.85% p.a.

Annual Expense Drag: ₹92,500 / year
Optimized Core Structure

6 Well-Defined Schemes (₹50,00,000)

Average weighted TER: 1.15% p.a.

Annual Expense Drag: ₹57,500 / year (₹35,000 saved)

What to do next: Action checklist

  • check_circle Generate a single Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) via CAMS or KFintech using your PAN.
  • check_circle Calculate cross-scheme stock overlap between large-cap and flexi-cap schemes.
  • check_circle Map capital gains against the annual ₹1.25 Lakh Section 112A exemption before triggering switches.
