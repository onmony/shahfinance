Scope of Practice

Core areas of portfolio guidance

01 Diagnostic

Consolidated Portfolio Review

Direct upload and evaluation of your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS: a single composite document of mutual fund holdings across CAMS and KFintech) to pinpoint duplicated funds, expense ratio drag, and category overlap.

Deliverable

A forensic overlap matrix detailing exact common stock exposure across all domestic equity schemes.

02 Restructuring

Tax-Aware Allocation

Restructuring mutual fund asset allocation across equity, hybrid, and debt schemes to optimize tax efficiency under current Indian capital gains rules, balancing long-term compounding against liquidity needs.

Deliverable

A streamlined asset allocation map aligned with the latest Section 112A capital gains tax provisions.

03 Risk Mitigation

ESOPs & Concentrated Equity Diversification

Disciplined phased reinvestment of stock options and executive bonuses into diversified mutual fund baskets, preventing family balance sheets from over-relying on a single sector or employer.

Deliverable

A time-staged Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) roadmap to unwind single-ticker risk systematically.

04 Permanence

Succession, Wills & Insurance Adequacy

Streamlining family asset titling, reviewing nomination records across historical folios, and conducting pure-risk term insurance adequacy audits to shield multi-generational wealth.

Deliverable

A comprehensive nominee validation report and mortality protection assessment.

Clear compensation, zero surprises
