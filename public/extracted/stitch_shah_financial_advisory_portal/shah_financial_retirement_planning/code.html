Knowledge Base

Frequently asked questions about retirement & SWP

1. What is an SWP and how does it compare to fixed deposits?

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) allows you to withdraw a pre-decided sum monthly from your mutual fund scheme while the remaining balance stays invested. Unlike bank fixed deposits where the entire interest payout is taxed annually at your slab rate, an SWP is treated as partial redemption, meaning tax applies only to the proportional capital gain portion.

2. How much can I safely withdraw each year without running out?

In Indian market conditions, an initial withdrawal rate of 3.5% to 4.2% of your corpus in Year 1, adjusted for inflation thereafter, has historically demonstrated sustainable durability over a 30-year span. Withdrawing higher rates without active asset rebalancing poses severe depletion risks during bear markets.

3. How does taxation work on mutual fund withdrawals in retirement?

Withdrawals from equity-oriented schemes held beyond 12 months attract Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax of 12.5% on gains exceeding ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year. Debt mutual funds purchased on or after April 1, 2023, are taxed as short-term capital gains at your applicable slab rate upon redemption.

4. What happens to my pension and EPF when I retire?

Accumulated Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) balances after five continuous years of service are generally tax-free upon retirement. We coordinate the tax-efficient transfer of EPF and National Pension System (NPS) maturity proceeds into structured mutual fund allocations to generate continuous cash flow.

5. Can returning NRIs maintain overseas bank accounts while retiring in Hyderabad?

Yes, under the Reserve Bank of India's Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) provisions, individuals who return to India can maintain overseas bank accounts and investments acquired while abroad. You can also transfer foreign funds into a Resident Foreign Currency (RFC) account in Hyderabad without restriction.

6. How do we budget for rising private healthcare costs in Hyderabad?

Healthcare inflation in Hyderabad private hospitals typically trends between 10% and 14% annually. We structure an independent liquid reserve specifically designated for critical care coverage, paired with comprehensive senior citizen health insurance to avoid liquidating long-term equity growth assets during medical emergencies.

7. How often will our retirement plan be reviewed and adjusted?

We conduct structured semi-annual reviews to evaluate cash-flow adequacy, rebalance between asset buckets, and align your distribution with evolving tax rules. In addition, clients have access to our Begumpet desk for unscheduled check-ins when lifestyle expenses or family needs shift.

8. How do we ensure our nominations and wealth transfer to our children remain smooth?

Every mutual fund folio allows up to three registered nominees with specified percentage shares. We review joint holding mandates (either or survivor) and nomination registries across your folios to ensure seamless asset succession for overseas or resident children.
